01 · About

A developer who learns by building

I'm currently in my first year of a Bachelor of Computer Applications at the School of Management Sciences, Lucknow, holding an 8.27 CGPA. Alongside my degree, I'm working through the MERN stack — MongoDB, Express, React, and Node — and applying every new concept directly to real projects instead of leaving it as theory.

That approach took shape during a MERN Stack internship at Infoseek Pvt. Ltd., where I got my first real exposure to how front-end and back-end pieces fit together in production-style code. Since then I've kept building: an expense tracker with authentication, a freelancer invoice tracker, and a campus lost-and-found platform are all in active development.

Outside of code, I sing, and I treat continuous learning as a habit rather than a milestone — which is why networking and server administration are next on my list.
